Cadence is a Python/AppKit desktop lyric companion by Edward Link.

The Apple Music AppleScript bridge, song-derived length-weighted word timing,
and private SkyLight lock-screen space bridge were adapted from Maestro by Shreeshanth R:
https://github.com/Maestro2903/maestro-lyrics
The original MIT notice is retained in MAESTRO-LICENSE.

Verso inspired the album-colored backdrop and desktop typography visually.
No Verso source code, assets, or shaders are included.

Lyrics and album artwork remain the property of their respective owners.
Apple Music's local cache, LRCLIB, NetEase, and Apple's iTunes Search service
supply these at runtime.

The read-only Apple Music cache lookup and TTML parsing approach is adapted from
Apple Music Lyrics (MIT), https://github.com/Takpap/apple-music-lyrics.
Its license is retained in APPLE-MUSIC-LYRICS-LICENSE. No cached song data is included.
